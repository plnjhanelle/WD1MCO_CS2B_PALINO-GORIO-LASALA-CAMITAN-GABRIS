Movie Synopsis & Overview

Tanjiro Kamado and his comrades of the Demon Slayer Corps take on a new mission aboard the mysterious Mugen Train, where over 40 people have vanished without a trace. Joined by Kyojuro Rengoku, the Flame Hashira, they team up to defeat a demon who has trapped the passengers in a profound, inescapable slumber.

Plunged into idealized dreamscapes reflecting their deepest desires, the slayers must uncover the truth before the train becomes their moving coffin, leading up to an earth-shattering confrontation with Upper Rank Three.
